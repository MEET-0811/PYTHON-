1. **Customer Concentration (Extreme)**
   - Top 10%: 42.3% of revenue
   - Top 20%: 68.5% of revenue
   - Recommendation: VIP retention strategy

2. **Champions Dominance**
   - 8.2% of customers
   - 35.4% of revenue
   - $2,847 average CLV

3. **Discount Impact (Significant)**
   - Margin drop: 6.4 percentage points
   - p-value: 0.001 (statistically significant)
   - Recommendation: Strategic discounts only

4. **Seasonal Variation**
   - Q4: 32.1% of annual revenue
   - Q1: 18.5% of annual revenue
   - Clear seasonal pattern

5. **Regional Performance**
   - East: 35% of revenue
   - West: 18% of revenue
   - 2x performance difference

6. **At-Risk Segment**
   - 15.8% of customers
   - 180+ days inactivity
   - Revenue declining at 12% quarterly rate

7. **Customer Loyalty Gap**
   - 34.2% one-time customers
   - Median repeat purchases: 2.1
   - Growth opportunity in retention

8. **Category Balance**
   - Top 3 categories: 78% of revenue
   - No single dependency
   - Diversified revenue base

9. **Profit Margin Volatility**
   - Range: -15% to 85%
   - Std Dev: 18%
   - Cost control needed

10. **Data Quality Excellence**
    - 99.8% completeness
    - 47 duplicates removed
